Kitchen hacks

Habits that hold up

  1. Dry meat or tofu before you sear it. Surface water steams instead of browning.
  2. Salt pasta water until it tastes lightly salty. The pasta seasons as it cooks.
  3. Peel ginger with the edge of a spoon. It follows the knobs better than a peeler.
  4. Keep a hard-cheese rind for the next pot of beans or broth, then fish it out.
  5. Put woody herb stems in the pot and pick them out before serving.
  6. Heat the pan, then add the oil, then add the food.
  7. A splash of leftover pickle brine can season a dressing. Taste as you go; it is already salty.
  8. Cool leftover rice quickly and refrigerate it within about an hour. Reheat it until piping hot. Rice left warm for hours can make people ill.
