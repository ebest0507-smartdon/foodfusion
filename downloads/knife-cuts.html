Five knife cuts worth practising

Use a sharp knife and a stable board. A dull knife slips. Even pieces matter more than speed.

Julienne

Slice, stack, then cut matchsticks about 3mm wide. Useful for quick-cooking vegetables.

Dice

Cut across the matchsticks to make cubes. A medium dice is about 1cm.

Chiffonade

Roll soft leaves tightly and slice across the roll for ribbons.

Brunoise

A fine dice made from a fine julienne. Take your time.

Bias cut

Slice carrots or spring onions on a strong angle so more surface hits the pan.
