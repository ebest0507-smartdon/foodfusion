Jollof rice

55 minutes · 6 servings · Amara Okonkwo

One pot of long-grain rice cooked in a tomato, onion, and thyme base until the grains are separate and the colour runs deep orange.

Ingredients

  • 400g long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, blended or finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes, blended
  • 1 red pepper, blended
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 600ml hot stock or water
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the oil, then cook the tomato paste until the oil turns red.
  2. Add tomatoes, pepper, garlic, and spices. Simmer until the sauce thickens, about 15 minutes.
  3. Stir in the rice and hot stock. Cover tightly and cook on low heat for 20 to 25 minutes.
  4. Rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff gently before serving.
