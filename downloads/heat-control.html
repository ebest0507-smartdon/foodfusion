Heat, lids, and resting

  • Low heat sweats onions until soft. If they colour fast, the pan is too hot.
  • A simmer is small bubbles, not a rolling boil. Stews and barley stay intact.
  • A hard sear needs a dry surface and room in the pan. Crowding steams the food.
  • A lid traps steam and shortens the time water takes to boil. Leave it ajar if you want the liquid to reduce.
  • Resting lets meat finish and the juices settle. A pot of rice can finish off the heat, lid on, for about 10 minutes.

If the browned bits in a pan are black, do not scrape them into a sauce. That bitterness will not cook out.
