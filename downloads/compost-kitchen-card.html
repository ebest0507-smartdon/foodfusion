What goes in a small home compost bin

Yes

Vegetable peels, coffee grounds, tea leaves, crushed eggshells, and plain cooked rice or pasta in small amounts if your council or bin allows food waste.

Go carefully

Lots of citrus, oil, and wet scraps can turn a small bin slimy. Mix in dry cardboard or leaves so it is not a sealed puddle.

Leave out of an open backyard pile

Meat, fish, and dairy attract pests in a small open bin. Plastic, tea bags with plastic mesh, and glossy paper do not compost.

Follow your local food-waste collection if you have one. It handles more than a backyard pile can.
